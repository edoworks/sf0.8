description: Pressure-test a decision with charitable questions, evidence, and falsifiable tests
agent: general
---

Apply `curiosity-audit`. Restate the strongest version of the claim, separate
evidence from inference and unknowns, ask high-value questions, preserve
disagreement, propose a falsifiable test, and state what would change the
recommendation. Keep the review advisory and human-gated. Refuse requests to
imitate a named person or ingest their media; use the neutral method instead.
