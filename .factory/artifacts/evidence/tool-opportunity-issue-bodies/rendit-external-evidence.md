# Evaluate External Evidence for Rendit Workflow Opportunity

## Goal

Determine whether independent users experience the deterministic asset
rendering, provenance, reproducibility, or review problem represented by the
Rendit shovel signal, and whether existing alternatives carry economic value.

## Acceptance Criteria

- State audience, jurisdiction, date cutoff, source plan, and stopping rule.
- Use primary or credible secondary sources; treat vendor marketing as context,
  not customer evidence.
- Separate facts, evidence, inference, assumptions, and unknowns.
- Search both supporting evidence and strongest counterclaims.
- Record existing alternatives, observable resource expenditure, and pricing or
  labor evidence without inventing demand or TAM.
- Update the ledger only with cited evidence and preserve `UNKNOWN` where gaps
  remain.

## Stop Condition

Stop when remaining uncertainty requires interviews, observed workflow use,
payment, or founder authorization. Do not contact users, publish, package, or
start product implementation.

## Decision Boundary

The result may move Rendit from internal infrastructure toward a better-founded
shovel hypothesis, but cannot authorize public packaging or product investment.
