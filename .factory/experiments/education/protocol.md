# Education Evidence Frontier Protocol

These are human-run falsification experiments, not product prototypes. Recruit
the named customer-zero type, preregister the narrow claim and stop rule, and
record what happened rather than what the participant says they might do.

Machine evidence can select and bound an experiment. It cannot count as customer,
learning, behavior-change, or payment evidence. A result is admissible only when
an adult researcher records an external human observation and the consent and
data-minimization fields are complete.

## Shared safeguards

- Use anonymous experiment IDs; do not record child names, birth dates, school,
  household details, persistent identifiers, audio, video, or unrelated behavior.
- Parent/guardian consent is required for a child participant and for any
  parent-recorded observation. Teacher permission is required for anonymized
  student artifacts; do not copy student identifiers into factory records.
- Store only task responses, adult rescue/intervention observations, timestamps
  needed for the protocol, and the participant's chosen payment signal.
- Stop if distress, unsafe content, privacy uncertainty, or answer leakage occurs.

## Evidence classes

Record machine-obtainable evidence separately from external human evidence. The
frontier is reached when research can no longer resolve the remaining claim
without observing a real person, real workflow, learning outcome, behavior
change, or transaction.
