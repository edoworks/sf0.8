## Subject Lock

Should Foculoom invest in a child-oriented deterministic runner whose future
runs adapt to observed play preferences?

This package does not authorize a product, revive Skiplet, select an engine, or
permit implementation beyond the experiment defined in
[ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). The candidate constitution and PRD become product
authority only after that experiment passes and the owner assigns a canonical
product repository.

## Decision Summary

**Invest in one movement-first experiment, not the proposed full product.**

The opportunity is a replayable mastery toy with expressive movement,
discoverable authored surprises, and persistent memories of accomplishments.
The opportunity is not another three-lane runner, an infinite content machine,
or an AI showcase. Current evidence does not establish that personalization
improves fun. It earns further investment only after the non-adaptive game
passes the fun, replayability, retention, and meaningful-variety gates.

## Living Sections

### Product Truths

- Movement quality precedes progression, procedural generation, adaptation, and AI.
- Gameplay correctness is deterministic and engine-authoritative.
- Generated content is untrusted until schema, safety, difficulty, and simulation checks pass.
- The complete game must work without a generative model, account, or network.
- Adaptation may serve player agency and accessibility; it may not optimize compulsion.
- Child privacy, clear stopping points, and fair monetization outrank retention metrics.

### Current Hypotheses

- A one-input movement system can support expressive mastery across ages 6-12.
- Authored chunks composed deterministically can provide meaningful rather than cosmetic variety.
- Story-worthy gameplay callbacks can create anticipation without dialogue generation.
- Local, confidence-bounded task-selection evidence can improve selections without changing rules.
- A premium or trial-plus-unlock model can earn parent trust, but commercial demand is unknown.

### Experiments

- `E0`: movement bake-off defined in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).

### Validated Systems

- None. Existing code establishes technical precedent, not player enjoyment.

### Rejected Ideas

- Starting with an AI director.
- Hidden dynamic difficulty or rules changes.
- Arbitrary runtime content generation.
- Live-ops obligations, streak loss, loot boxes, paid revives, consumable boosts, and behavioral advertising.
- Bosses, crafting, habitat building, dialogue systems, social feeds, and UGC before core fun is proven.

### Future Bets

- Algorithmic adaptation after meaningful variety is demonstrated.
- Foundation Models-assisted offline plan ideation after the algorithmic director is a measured baseline.
- Expansion packs after demand and content economics are measured.
