# Design Intent

Complete this short record before substantial user-facing implementation. It is
an intent contract, not a style guide. Empty answers are a design decision that
should be visible, not silently filled by implementation.

- **Who is this for?**
- **What should they feel?**
- **What should they notice first?**
- **What is the primary action or experience?**
- **What can disappear?**
- **What should move?**
- **What should make sound?**
- **What should feel physical?**
- **What should feel magical?**
- **What should require zero explanation?**
- **What makes this distinctive?**
- **Which current Apple capabilities could improve it?**

The review record must also name rendered evidence, known design debt, and Apple
platform debt. A capability is not adopted because it is new; it needs a
product-specific reason and a deployment-target-aware disposition.
