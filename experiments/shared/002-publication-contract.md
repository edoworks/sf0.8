# ChatGPT Sites Publication Contract

Use this addendum with every site update prompt.

Publication mode: [DRAFT or PUBLIC]

In DRAFT mode:

- Placeholder URLs are permitted.
- No real data collection may be implied.
- Unavailable features must be labeled planned, illustrative, or coming soon.
- The page must not imply that a beta, audit request, download, or signup is currently available.

In PUBLIC mode:

- Placeholder URLs are forbidden.
- Every primary CTA must have a verified HTTPS destination.
- If a destination is unavailable, remove the CTA or replace it with clearly labeled non-functional text.
- Do not display a form unless submission has been tested end-to-end.
- Do not claim open source unless the source repository is publicly reachable.
- Do not claim audit availability unless the request path reaches a monitored destination.
- Do not add analytics unless its processor, consent behavior, fields, retention, and privacy notice are specified.
- Do not invent facts, capabilities, testimonials, metrics, or legal claims.

Before publishing, verify:

- No unresolved placeholder tokens remain.
- Every visible link works.
- The primary journey works on mobile and desktop.
- Keyboard navigation and basic accessibility work.
- Approved disclaimers remain visible.
- Privacy and contact paths are present.
- The page states whether it is a concept, beta, or available product.

The site is a disposable experiment deployment. The experiment issue, prompt version, approved claims, public URL, and verification result are the factory record.
