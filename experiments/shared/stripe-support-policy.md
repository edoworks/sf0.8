unlock software, beta access, assessments, reports, audits, rankings, or any
other entitlement. The public sites must remain usable without payment.

## Operational responsibilities

- Stripe hosts checkout and processes the payment information; the public site
  does not collect card data.
- The owner must confirm Stripe receipt behavior and support contact details in
  the Stripe account before making any receipt or support promise in site copy.
- The owner handles refund requests according to the Stripe account and
  applicable law; the sites must not promise refunds that have not been
  approved and configured.
- The owner is responsible for tax registration, reporting, and any applicable
  sales-tax, VAT, donation, or contribution treatment. Stripe settings are not
  a substitute for professional tax advice.
- Payment-method and country availability can vary by customer, product, and
  Stripe eligibility. “Worldwide” access to the free experiences must not be
  represented as universal payment availability.
- Any support request must use a monitored owner-controlled contact path before
  the sites claim that donor support is available.

## Publication guard

Before changing these links or adding new support copy, verify the public URL,
active state, amount, product name, optional/non-gating behavior, receipt
behavior, refund handling, support contact, and regional availability. Never
store Stripe API credentials in this repository or in a ChatGPT Sites prompt.
