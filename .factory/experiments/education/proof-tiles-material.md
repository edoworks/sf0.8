# Proof Tiles Material

Use paper cards or a local static sequence for one narrow fraction concept.
Prepare preregistered `BEFORE`, `EXPERIENCE`, `IMMEDIATE_AFTER`, `NOVEL_TRANSFER`,
and `48_HOUR_RETEST` tasks. Do not reveal the rule before the learner predicts,
manipulates, and explains. The adult records rescue attempts and the learner's
independent actions. Ask for the next concept only after the second attempt and
record the strongest actual payment signal.
