# Participant Script

Use plain language and read only the section for the selected experiment.

1. “We are testing this task, not you. You may stop at any time.”
2. “Please do the task as you normally would. I will not coach unless the
   protocol says so.”
3. “I will record what happened, not a name or recording.”
4. At the end: “Would you independently ask to do this again, refer someone,
   request access, leave a deposit, or purchase it? Please choose only what you
   actually did or are willing to do now; ‘interested’ is not a purchase.”

For child sessions, obtain adult consent first, let the adult end the session,
and ask the adult to record observations rather than collecting child identity.
