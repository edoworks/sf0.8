# Mews & Woofs Creation Prompt

Source: user-provided prompt, preserved verbatim for provenance.

Create a warm, playful, mobile-first public website for Mews & Woofs, an iOS app that helps people listen to and playfully interpret their pets’ sounds and behavior.

Purpose:
Explain the product clearly, collect qualified beta interest, and direct interested visitors to the app’s download or testing link.

Audience:
Pet owners who enjoy understanding their cats and dogs better and want a playful, non-clinical way to reflect on pet sounds and behavior.

Core message:
Mews & Woofs turns moments with your pet into playful interpretations and memorable listening experiences. It is entertainment and reflection, not veterinary diagnosis.

Build these sections:

1. Hero
- Headline: “What is your pet trying to tell you?”
- Explain the app in one or two sentences.
- Primary CTA: “Join the beta”
- Secondary CTA: “See how it works”

2. How it works
Show a simple three-step flow:
- Listen to a pet sound
- Add context about the moment
- Receive a playful interpretation

Clearly state that interpretations are not medical, behavioral, or veterinary advice.

3. Pet profiles
Explain that users can create profiles for individual pets.
Include examples of profile details:
- Name
- Species
- Personality
- Relevant context
- Listening history

Do not request sensitive personal information.

4. Example interpretation cards
Create three fictional examples:
- Cat vocalization
- Dog sound
- Ambiguous or uncertain sound

Each example must be labeled as playful and illustrative, not scientifically verified.

5. Why people use it
Use honest benefits:
- Notice patterns in pet behavior
- Create a fun shared ritual
- Keep memorable interpretation cards
- Learn more about each pet’s individual personality

Do not claim that the app translates animal language or detects illness.

6. Beta signup
Create a simple form with:
- Name, optional
- Email, required
- Pet species
- Optional message
- Consent checkbox for receiving beta updates

Include a privacy note and a placeholder form destination:
[BETA_FORM_URL]

7. Download or testing CTA
Add placeholders:
- App Store → [APP_STORE_URL]
- TestFlight → [TESTFLIGHT_URL]

If links are unavailable, use “Coming soon” rather than inventing URLs.

8. FAQ
Answer:
- Does it literally translate pets?
- Is it veterinary advice?
- Which pets are supported?
- Is my pet’s audio stored?
- How can I join the beta?

Use cautious language and mark unanswered storage/privacy details as “To be confirmed.”

9. Footer
Include Mews & Woofs, Edoworks, privacy, contact, and beta links.

Design:
- Friendly, memorable, and calm
- Use a distinctive visual identity inspired by soft sound waves, pet profiles, and listening
- Avoid childish stock imagery and generic AI branding
- Support desktop and mobile layouts
- Use accessible text sizes, contrast, keyboard navigation, and descriptive labels
- Do not collect pet medical information
- Do not collect payment-card information
- Do not include fabricated reviews, download counts, scientific claims, or testimonials

Before publishing, test the signup flow, consent checkbox, all links, responsive layout, accessibility, and all disclaimers.
Use placeholders for missing URLs and facts. Do not invent product capabilities.
Replace the bracketed URLs before publishing. For the first experiments, keep Rung focused on audit leads and Mews & Woofs focused on beta signups, rather than adding multiple monetization options at once.
