# Recommended Execution Order

1. **Proof Tiles**: cheapest paper/local artifact, low safety burden, reversible,
   and directly tests the strongest learning assumption plus adult rescue and a
   real one-time payment action.
2. **Side-by-Side Spark**: only after a family with a recent real homework
   friction is recruited; observation burden is higher but still no software.
3. **Misconception Lens**: manual concierge after teacher artifact permission;
   higher privacy and recruitment cost, but tests workflow and changed action
   without building integration.

This is an information-value sequence, not an attractiveness ranking. If the
first experiment falsifies the shared independent-learning assumption, stop and
reassess rather than executing the remaining kits automatically.
