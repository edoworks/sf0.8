# Adversarial Design Critic

Use after a meaningful surface is rendered and functional. The critic is a
subtraction pass, not an idea generator.

Ask, with screenshots or a recording in view:

- What attracts attention first, and is that correct?
- What looks interactive but is not, or is interactive but does not look it?
- What feels generic, like programmer art, or like an LLM template?
- What can be removed without reducing the experience?
- Is motion explaining origin, causality, state, hierarchy, or physicality?
- Are typography, symbols, hit targets, contrast, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, and
  Reduce Motion behaving as intended?
- Does the product have a specific emotional payoff and a recognizable identity?

Record each finding as `keep`, `remove`, `refine`, or `experiment`, with a
rendered-evidence path and a concrete next action. Do not use Apple Design Award
claims as an acceptance criterion.
